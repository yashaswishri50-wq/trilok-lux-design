# Hotel Trilok Brand Kit Alignment

## Direction
Rework the complete guest-facing website to follow the supplied Hotel Trilok brand kit rather than the current gold-led interpretation. Preserve the working booking and sign-in flows while making the presentation consistently sophisticated, serene, timeless, and recognizably Trilok.

## Changes
1. **Exact brand system**
   - Replace the current palette with the documented Midnight Blue `#162B56`, Silver `#C9CCD3`, and Ivory `#F5F3EF` roles.
   - Use Cormorant Garamond for premium editorial headings, Trajan-style typography for taglines and formal accents, and Inter for body copy and controls.
   - Remove logo blend modes, glows, shadows, distortion, and undersized uses; preserve the approved logo proportions and clear space.

2. **Brand-kit homepage**
   - Align the header and first view with the brand-kit website direction: horizontal logo, formal navigation, `HOTEL TRILOK`, `THREE WORLDS. ONE HOSPITALITY.`, refined supporting copy, dominant booking action, and the working booking form.
   - Restyle every section, card, button, form, divider, and sticky action using the official navy/silver/ivory system.
   - Retain rooms, dining, wellness, celebrations, offers, gallery, reviews, contact, and the welcome kit, but revise overstated copy to the kit's calm, polished tone.

3. **Photography and amenities**
   - Apply the documented quiet-luxury photography treatment: muted, soft, natural, warm-tinted, and low contrast.
   - Replace the generic welcome-kit presentation with appropriate branded amenity imagery extracted from the supplied kit where it improves accuracy.

4. **Booking and account experience**
   - Keep the real calendar, room/guest selection, on-screen confirmation, authentication, profile, and booking history fully working.
   - Carry the same official colors, typography, logo handling, and voice across sign-in, password reset, and account pages.

5. **Verification**
   - Check the homepage and guest-account screens on desktop and mobile for logo clarity, color accuracy, typography hierarchy, readable contrast, working navigation, booking interaction, and visual consistency.

## Technical notes
- The PDF supplies visual guidance but not a licensed Trajan Pro font file; use `Trajan Pro` when installed and a close web-safe classic-capitals fallback otherwise.
- No booking, authentication, or database behavior will be removed or redesigned beyond its visual brand alignment.

# TRILOK Website Enhancement Plan

## Direction
Preserve the existing TRILOK visual identity: deep navy/ink surfaces, gold accents, Cormorant Garamond display type, cinematic photography, and the current logo treatment. The requested new hotel and celebration content will be added without changing to the proposed light theme.

## Changes
1. **Cinematic hero slideshow**
   - Replace the single hero image with an automatic full-width slideshow featuring rooms, reception, dining, wedding halls, and celebrations.
   - Add restrained crossfades, image labels, progress indicators, and previous/next controls.
   - Keep the TRILOK logo, primary booking actions, and booking widget prominent and readable on every slide.

2. **Rooms and booking**
   - Present Deluxe Rooms, Premium Rooms, and Suites as the main stay categories.
   - Use the temporary pricing format `From ₹X/night` rather than invented rates.
   - Keep `BOOK YOUR STAY` and `CHECK AVAILABILITY` as the dominant stay actions, including the mobile booking bar.

3. **Weddings and celebrations**
   - Expand the current events area into a major visual feature.
   - Showcase wedding halls, party halls, birthdays, engagements, receptions, family functions, and corporate events.
   - Provide separate `PLAN YOUR WEDDING` and `ENQUIRE FOR EVENTS` actions that lead to the enquiry area.

4. **Hotel experiences**
   - Retain and refine restaurant, high tea, lounge, gym, spa, reception/waiting area, amenities, offers, gallery, reviews, location, contact, and social links.
   - Adjust copy and image presentation to feel warmer and more distinctly Indian while staying within the established theme.

5. **Responsive polish and verification**
   - Ensure slideshow controls, booking fields, event actions, cards, and sticky booking access work cleanly on desktop and mobile.
   - Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve accessible labels, image descriptions, and keyboard-operable controls.
   - Verify the live page at desktop and mobile sizes and check for layout or browser errors.

## Technical notes
- Reuse the existing image set where suitable and add cohesive celebration/reception imagery only where the current assets do not cover the requested subjects.
- Keep the current single-page structure and existing booking/enquiry feedback behavior; no database or payment system is included.
- Preserve the existing placeholder contact details until real hotel details are supplied.
